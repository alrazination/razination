FAQ

Before you ask.

Will it run in our LMS?

Almost certainly. SCORM 1.2, SCORM 2004 or xAPI, tested in your LMS before handover. No LMS? We can host it.

What do you need from us?

One expert for a kick-off call and two short reviews, the material you already have, and ideally a few learners for a play-test.

Do you collect learner data?

No. The simulations collect nothing unless you ask for tracking. With tracking, your LMS records completion and score.

Who owns it?

You do. You get the source files and short notes on how to change text and images. It runs without us.

Does it work on phones?

Videos, activities and most modules do. Detailed drag-and-drop simulations are built for laptops and tablets first. We tell you up front what fits.

Can it be in German?

Ask for it in your quote. Extra languages are priced in up front. The German kitchen game on the simulations page is one we built.
